Rules this design follows

  • The "no reply means yes" part covers the values only. The member corrects, or doesn't. Sending their data to The Zebra is still one deliberate tap, with the disclosure right under it.
  • Every guess is labeled as a guess on screen, not hidden.
  • We ask outright only when we can't make a sensible guess: the car, and credit where it counts.
