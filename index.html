All three side by side

Same person, same platform. The differences are whether payroll is connected, and whether we piggyback Zebra fields onto steps she's already doing.

What Finch actually buys us

  • Zero typing in onboarding. Name, birthday, state and ZIP are already there; Maya just confirms.
  • 3 fields nobody else gives us: gender and full home street address (garaging address) from /individual, and work location from /employment. Work location vs. home is what lets us estimate mileage and commute use.
  • Result: the "last stretch" drops from 6 questions (one is typing a street address) to 2 taps (education and tickets) plus 2 pre-filled confirms.
  • Accuracy: payroll DOB and address are verified data. Typos in self-typed info change the quote.

What we need to build to get here

  1. Onboarding S2: also pull gender, street address and work location from Finch (today the prototype only uses state + ZIP).
  2. My Financial Life → Zebra card: if Maya picked a carrier on her Auto Insurance tile, pre-answer "currently insured: yes".
  3. Zebra card: pass ZIP, street address and email through, and decode the VIN for submodel.
  4. New "last stretch" step: education, tickets/accidents, and confirm the estimated mileage + use. Never default tickets.
  5. Consent: make the "Get my quotes" tap the consent, with the disclosure beside it (counsel to review).
  6. Confirm with Fritz which event pays us, and that prepop accepts all 21 fields.
