How to get to revenue faster

What this lab shows, plus where the lines are on synthetic personas and negative consent.

Do now · free fields

We already have about 10 of the 21

Name, email, DOB, state and ZIP come from payroll. Marital status and own/rent come from onboarding. Gender and the full garaging address are also in Finch's /individual record; we just don't use them yet. Switching that on adds 2 fields with zero extra questions.
Do now · estimate, then confirm

Mileage + primary use from the commute

Home address vs. work location gives a solid annual-mileage estimate (commute × 2 × workdays + ~5,000 personal miles), and "commute" vs. "pleasure" follows from remote/on-site status. The member sees it already filled in and only touches it if it's wrong. That turns 2 questions into 0.
Big unlock · build next

"Snap your insurance card"

The car is the one thing nothing can derive. A photo of the ID card or declarations page gives us year, make, model, trim/VIN, insured status, and owned/financed (from the lienholder). The Vault already does document reading (pdf.js + Tesseract). Turn it on above and watch the car questions go away.
Use with care

"Negative consent" for answers: yes, mostly

Pre-filling answers the member can change is a normal, legitimate pattern, and it's the fastest version of this flow. Two limits:
  • Never default the driving record (violations). If we send "no tickets" and they have one, the rate jumps when they buy, they don't convert, and our payout is at risk. It's also misrepresentation on an insurance application.
  • Education and credit defaults are lower risk but still change the rate. The lab flags anything untouched in amber so you can see how much of the record is "assumed."
Don't ship

Negative consent for sharing / contact: no

Pre-checked "share my info / OK to call me" boxes generally don't count as valid consent. TCPA calls and texts need an affirmative act, and state privacy rules plus the FTC treat pre-checked boxes as a dark pattern. Weak consent also undermines the lead Zebra is paying for.

Same speed, real consent: the button the member already has to tap ("Get my quotes") is the consent, with the disclosure right next to it. That's the "One-tap button" mode above, and it costs zero extra taps. Have counsel check the final wording.
Test only

Synthetic personas: great for testing, never for production

  • Yes: clicking through, demoing to Fritz, and sending the 5 personas through Zebra's QA/UAT environment (Fritz confirmed there's a separate token per environment) to prove all 21 fields round-trip and attribution shows up.
  • Never: submitting synthetic people to production. That's a fabricated lead: clawbacks, a terminated contract, and possible fraud exposure.

Questions to send Fritz (these decide where the "we get paid" button actually sits)

  1. What exact event triggers our compensation: the prepop hand-off, the member viewing quotes, a carrier click, or a bind? How much per event?
  2. Does /api/ext/v1/prepop/ accept all 21 RC1 fields? Eric was told there's no user_responses structure, and today we only send first/last/email/phone/state. We need the full accepted field list.
  3. Gender: can we send "unspecified" or omit it in states that ban gender rating (CA, HI, MA, MI, MT, NC, PA), or for members whose payroll record says "decline to specify"?
  4. Credit: you said it isn't required in CA/HI/MA. Does a lead without it still count as "complete" there? Is the ballpark range we collect enough everywhere else?
  5. Is a pre-filled answer the member reviewed treated the same as one they typed?
  6. Can we run synthetic test personas through QA, and will those show up in attribution/commission reporting so we can verify tracking end to end?
